- instruction-gap: "every seen screw head at least 3 px", with "seen" meaning any vertex in view, fails for a screw that straddles the screen edge. With the gauge's place fixed, the bezel's elbow-side corner screws sit right on the bottom edge: the inner one's centre is at NDC y -1.009, so 1.9 px of its head shows. No position inside the 5 mm bezel border clears that. I put the two elbow-side screws on the bezel's elbow-side wall by its corners (out of view; 2 of 4 are seen, as the spec allows), and the two wrist-side ones on the face. A future spec with small parts near a screen edge should say whether a part cut by the edge counts, or measure only parts wholly in view.
- instruction-gap: "the face the first thing a ray from the eye toward each face vertex meets" gave no tolerance. Rays that graze the dial's rim run on and hit the housing behind the vertex, which a literal check counts as blocked (13 vertices on the first run). I count only hits more than 0.5 mm in front of the vertex (the same tolerance as "seen") and recorded that in assets.md.
- instruction-gap: a knurled dial "on the bezel's inner side (toward the body, engine -X)" whose axis points -X faces the eye at cos 0.2, which squeezes the 16-ridge knurl pitch well under 3 px. I mounted it beside the inner side on a boss and turned its face 75% toward the eye (pitch 3.73-4.90 px).
- instruction-gap: the Assets triangle guide (hands about 5,000, glove detail about 3,000, compass about 2,500) doesn't fit the stitching the same section sets. 5 mm dashes 2.5 mm apart along both sides of every finger and round the cuff come to 260 dashes, 3,120 triangles, on their own. I kept the stitching as specified and the hands (3,080) and compass (896) lighter, at 15,844 in all. A spec's guide should be worked from its own counts.
- doc-gap: detail on a blended surface (the cuff) drifted off it in the wrist test poses: 60 stitch dashes failed the attachment check. The cause is that skinning interpolates the facet linearly between its ring vertices' weights, while evaluating the blend function at the detail's own position gives different weights. Taking the weights by interpolating between the rings' weights fixed it (`cuff_weights` in build_arms.py). Nothing in the art style docs says this. It belongs with "stitches and patches take the weights of the surface under them".
- doc-gap: Workbench's default view transform (AgX in Blender 5.2) washes the previews out, so the 160/146/120 grime bands looked the same as the 206/194/168 wraps. I found it with a flat-lit test render. The previews now use the Standard view transform. The desert art style's Delivery section could say so.
- tool: the attachment check built a BVH from the whole mesh for every piece, which doesn't scale to 511 pieces. It now builds each piece's tree from that piece's own vertices (`attachment_problems` in desert/tools/blender/build_arms.py, extended, not copied). The perspective helpers `screen_width`, `first_hit` and `seen` sit beside `project`/`in_view` in the same file. Both are still per-script; a shared tools/blender/ module for view-model checks would serve the next character.
- tool: docs/tasks/desert-arms-detail/scripts/compare_joints.py compares two glbs' joint names, node transforms and inverse bind matrices, to prove a rebuild kept the rig. build_only.py (triangles by part, without checks) and probe.py (compass first-hit and screw extents) are one-off diagnostics.
